# Mindcast Kids — Picture Book List

*One book per week, matched to the child track theme. 52 titles, no repeats.*

---

## Before you source the read-alouds

**Most YouTube picture-book read-alouds are unauthorised uploads.** Using one in a members-only paid session is a different thing from a parent watching one at home, and it sits awkwardly against Mindcast's own sourcing standard — third-party video is linked, never re-hosted.

Three safer routes, in order:

1. **Buy the book and read it aloud in the room.** Best experience anyway — you can hold it up, pause, and let children answer. Budget roughly $25–35 a title, or borrow from Taupō Library.
2. **Use publisher or author channels.** Many of these have official readings — Penguin Kids, HarperKids, Storyline Online (SAG-AFTRA Foundation, fully licensed), and several authors read their own.
3. **Only then a general read-aloud upload**, and mark the `Read-Aloud Source Check` column so you know which weeks are unverified.

Storyline Online is worth checking first — it is free, licensed, professionally read, and covers a number of the titles below.

## Movement 1 · See Clearly

**Week 1 — The Signal and the Noise**

*The Quiet Book* — Deborah Underwood

Draw out: There are many different kinds of quiet, and you have to stop to notice them.

Ask: **What kind of quiet do you like best?**

---

**Week 2 — The Stories We Carry**

*Chrysanthemum* — Kevin Henkes

Draw out: What people say about you can start to feel true, even when it isn't.

Ask: **Has anyone ever said something about you that wasn't true?**

---

**Week 3 — The Pattern Interrupt**

*When Sophie Gets Angry — Really, Really Angry* — Molly Bang

Draw out: The gap between feeling furious and doing something about it — Sophie goes outside instead.

Ask: **What helps you when you feel like Sophie?**

---

**Week 4 — What Your Body Is Telling You**

*Listening to My Body* — Gabi Garcia

Draw out: Sensations come before words. Your body tells you things first.

Ask: **Where do you feel it in your body when you're excited?**

---

**Week 5 — The Mirror — How We See Ourselves**

*I Like Myself!* — Karen Beaumont

Draw out: Liking yourself on the inside, whatever the outside is doing.

Ask: **What's something you like about yourself?**

---

**Week 6 — The Comparison Loop**

*Giraffes Can't Dance* — Giles Andreae

Draw out: Gerald compares himself to everyone else until he finds his own music.

Ask: **What are you good at that's different from your friends?**

---

**Week 7 — The Inner Critic**

*The Bad Seed* — Jory John

Draw out: A seed who believes he's bad because he's been told so — and starts to change the story.

Ask: **What does the unkind voice in your head say?**

---

**Week 8 — Beneath the Surface — Emotions as Data**

*The Colour Monster* — Anna Llenas

Draw out: Sorting the tangle — big feelings have names and each one is different.

Ask: **What colour is your feeling today?**

---

**Week 9 — The Wounds We Carry**

*The Rabbit Listened* — Cori Doerrfeld

Draw out: Everyone tries to fix Taylor. The rabbit just sits with him. That's what helps.

Ask: **Who do you go to when something hurts?**

---

**Week 10 — The Masks We Wear**

*Perfectly Norman* — Tom Percival

Draw out: Norman hides his wings under a coat so nobody sees. The coat gets heavy.

Ask: **What do you hide so people don't see it?**

---

**Week 11 — Seeing Others Clearly**

*The Invisible Boy* — Trudy Ludwig

Draw out: Brian is unseen until one person notices him.

Ask: **Who in your class might be feeling invisible?**

---

**Week 12 — The Habit Loop — How We Got Here**

*Interrupting Chicken* — David Ezra Stein

Draw out: A chicken who interrupts without ever deciding to — and slowly notices she does it.

Ask: **What do you always do without meaning to?**

---

**Week 13 — Integration — What We Now See**

*The Wonderful Things You Will Be* — Emily Winfield Martin

Draw out: Looking back and looking forward, without needing to know the ending.

Ask: **What have you found out about yourself this term?**

## Movement 2 · Unlearn

**Week 14 — The Permission You're Still Waiting For**

*What Do You Do With an Idea?* — Kobi Yamada

Draw out: Nobody gives you permission for your idea. You decide to look after it.

Ask: **What's your idea that you haven't told anyone?**

---

**Week 15 — Letting Go of Who You Were Supposed to Be**

*Red: A Crayon's Story* — Michael Hall

Draw out: A crayon labelled red who is actually blue. Everyone tries to fix him.

Ask: **Has anyone ever expected you to be something you're not?**

---

**Week 16 — The Forgiveness Loop**

*Enemy Pie* — Derek Munson

Draw out: Staying cross takes a lot of work. Spending a day together takes less.

Ask: **Who are you cross with, and how long have you been cross?**

---

**Week 17 — Forgiving Yourself**

*The Girl Who Never Made Mistakes* — Mark Pett & Gary Rubinstein

Draw out: Beatrice is terrified of getting it wrong — until she does, and the world doesn't end.

Ask: **What happened last time you got something wrong?**

---

**Week 18 — Rewiring the Critic — Building the Inner Coach**

*I Am Enough* — Grace Byers

Draw out: The kind, honest voice — what it sounds like when you're on your own side.

Ask: **What would a kind coach say to you?**

---

**Week 19 — Breaking the People-Pleasing Pattern**

*Don't Hug Doug* — Carrie Finison

Draw out: Doug doesn't like hugs, and that's allowed. Ask first, and no is a real answer.

Ask: **When did you say yes when you wanted to say no?**

---

**Week 20 — Unlearning Scarcity — There Is Enough**

*Stone Soup* — Jon J Muth

Draw out: Everyone thinks there isn't enough, until everyone brings one thing.

Ask: **What could you bring that would help everyone?**

---

**Week 21 — The Relationships That Shaped You**

*Wilfrid Gordon McDonald Partridge* — Mem Fox

Draw out: What we carry from the people who care for us, and what we give back.

Ask: **What have the grown-ups who look after you taught you?**

---

**Week 22 — Setting Down the Armour**

*Ruby Finds a Worry* — Tom Percival

Draw out: Ruby's worry grows because she keeps it to herself. It shrinks when she says it out loud.

Ask: **What do you keep to yourself that's getting heavy?**

---

**Week 23 — Grief and Growth — What Endings Make Possible**

*The Memory Tree* — Britta Teckentrup

Draw out: Fox's friends gather to remember him, and a tree grows from the remembering.

Ask: **What ended that you still think about?**

---

**Week 24 — Rewiring Fear — From Threat to Signal**

*Jabari Jumps* — Gaia Cornwall

Draw out: Jabari is scared of the diving board and jumps anyway. Fear is information, not an instruction.

Ask: **What are you scared of that you'd like to try?**

---

**Week 25 — The Stories That No Longer Serve**

*Not Quite Narwhal* — Jessie Sima

Draw out: Kelp has always been told what he is — and finds out the old story isn't the whole one.

Ask: **What did you used to believe about yourself that isn't true now?**

---

**Week 26 — Integration — What We Have Released**

*The Heart and the Bottle* — Oliver Jeffers

Draw out: A girl puts her heart in a bottle to keep it safe, and has to work out how to get it back.

Ask: **What have you put down this term?**

## Movement 4 · Live It

**Week 40 — From Self-Development to Service**

*Have You Filled a Bucket Today?* — Carol McCloud

Draw out: What you've grown is for other people. Filling someone's bucket fills yours.

Ask: **Who could you help this week?**

---

**Week 41 — Teaching What You've Learned**

*Mango, Abuela, and Me* — Meg Medina

Draw out: Two people teaching each other, both learning.

Ask: **What could you teach someone younger than you?**

---

**Week 42 — The Courage to Be Seen**

*Say Something!* — Peter H. Reynolds

Draw out: Being seen and heard — in whatever way is yours.

Ask: **What's true about you that you keep hidden?**

---

**Week 43 — Living Your Values Under Pressure**

*One* — Kathryn Otoshi

Draw out: Standing up when it costs something, and how one voice changes a room.

Ask: **When is it hardest to do the right thing?**

---

**Week 44 — Conflict as a Growth Practice**

*The Sandwich Swap* — Queen Rania Al Abdullah & Kelly DiPucchio

Draw out: Two best friends fall out over lunch, and staying in it makes them closer.

Ask: **What argument do you need to sort out?**

---

**Week 45 — Generosity as a Way of Being**

*Last Stop on Market Street* — Matt de la Peña

Draw out: CJ's nana gives without it costing her — and shows him how to see.

Ask: **What can you give that doesn't cost anything?**

---

**Week 46 — Enough — Living From Sufficiency**

*Those Shoes* — Maribeth Boelts

Draw out: Jeremy wants the shoes badly, and works out what he already has.

Ask: **What's already good that you'd stopped noticing?**

---

**Week 47 — The Long Game — Patience and Persistence**

*The Curious Garden* — Peter Brown

Draw out: Liam tends something that takes years to show. Roots before shoots.

Ask: **What are you practising that's still hard?**

---

**Week 48 — Leaving People Better Than You Found Them**

*Each Kindness* — Jacqueline Woodson

Draw out: Chloe doesn't take the chance, and it stays with her. Small moments ripple.

Ask: **Who made you feel good this week, and how?**

---

**Week 49 — Your Legacy — What You're Building Beyond Yourself**

*Miss Rumphius* — Barbara Cooney

Draw out: Make the world more beautiful. The lupins outlast her.

Ask: **What do you want people to remember about you?**

---

**Week 50 — Gratitude as a Practice, Not a Feeling**

*Thankful* — Eileen Spinelli

Draw out: Noticing specific, ordinary good things on purpose.

Ask: **What three good things happened today?**

---

**Week 51 — The Ongoing Practice — There Is No Arrival**

*Everyone Can Learn to Ride a Bicycle* — Chris Raschka

Draw out: Falling off and getting back on, again and again. There is no finish line.

Ask: **What will you keep practising?**

---

**Week 52 — Integration — A Year of Becoming**

*Oh, the Places You'll Go!* — Dr. Seuss

Draw out: A whole year, and the road carrying on past it.

Ask: **What can you do now that you couldn't a year ago?**

## Aotearoa alternatives

**Rebekah (Bex) Lipp lives in Taupō.** Her Wildling Books series with illustrator Craig Phillips covers five of these weeks, and *How Do I Feel?* won a Storylines Notable Book Award.
