## AI Use Policy
Mindcast uses AI tooling to build and improve systems, but member trust takes priority over convenience.
## Hard rules
- Never paste member journal/reflection text into a third-party model.
- Never paste safeguarding records, health information, staff personnel records or identifiable complaints into an AI tool.
- Never paste service-role keys, API secrets, `.env` files, credentials or private certificates into a prompt.
- Never use production personal data in AI-generated test fixtures or screenshots.
- Use synthetic data for development and training examples.
- Do not train third-party models on Mindcast member content.
- Obtain and record vendor settings/contractual position on model training and retention where an AI provider is used operationally.
## Approved uses
Generic coding questions, architecture discussion, copy editing without personal data, synthetic test generation, public-information research and curriculum drafting that does not expose member content.
## Product AI
Any member-facing AI insight feature must be opt-in, transparent about what data is processed, able to be disabled without losing the rest of the service, and covered by the privacy policy/data map.
## Review
New AI services require privacy/security review before real data is processed.
