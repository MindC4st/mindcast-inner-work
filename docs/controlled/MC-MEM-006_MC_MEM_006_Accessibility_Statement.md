## Accessibility Statement
Mindcast aims to make its website, app, printed material and live sessions usable by as many people as possible. Our working standard for digital experiences is **WCAG 2.2 AA**.
## What we commit to
- Body text and interactive controls meet appropriate contrast requirements.
- Every interactive element has a visible keyboard focus state.
- Core member flows can be completed by keyboard.
- Layouts remain usable at 320px width and 200% zoom without loss of content.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Forms use clear labels, fieldsets and legends where appropriate.
- Important information is not communicated by colour alone.
- Printed and live-session alternatives are available when the app is inaccessible or unavailable.
## Known gaps
Accessibility is an ongoing product requirement. Any known unresolved accessibility defects are tracked in the App QA Checklist and must be prioritised according to impact.
## Tell us when we have got it wrong
Members and guests may raise an accessibility issue through the published contact/complaints route. Raising an accessibility issue will not affect membership or participation.
## Review
This statement is reviewed annually and after any material change to the member experience. An independent accessibility audit should be commissioned as Mindcast scales.
