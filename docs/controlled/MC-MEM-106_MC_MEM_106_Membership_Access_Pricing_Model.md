## Commercial principle
Mindcast is a transparent for-profit business. There is no collection plate, tithe, expected donation or hidden contribution. The price of paid access is stated clearly and is intended to cover operating costs, reinvestment and profit.
## Sunday access model
- **Live Sunday session:** free to attend, subject to capacity and registration.
- **First visit:** recommended full interactive trial access at no charge so a new person can experience the product.
- **Returning non-member:** may attend the live session but does not receive continuing app access, saved interactive answers, digital workbook history or member-only tools.
- **Daily physical worksheet:** **$5 NZD** for a non-member who wants a take-home reflection sheet.
- **Paid member:** full interactive participation, digital workbook, saved reflections/history, progress/check-ins, member tools, all weekly worksheets and priority/guaranteed booking when capacity is constrained.
## Working launch pricing
- Founding Adult: **$29/week**, first 100 memberships, locked for 12 months.
- Standard Adult: **$35/week**.
- Teen: working target **$22/week**.
- Child add-on: working target **$15/week**.
- Family bundle: working target **$79/week** for two adults plus up to three children/teens.
- Supported/community membership: working target **$19/week**, eligibility/process to be approved before launch.
All amounts are working commercial assumptions until the founder and accountant approve GST treatment and final pricing.
## What the member pays for
The paid relationship is deliberately clear: the member is paying for the product and infrastructure around the room — interactive software, persistent personal journey, worksheets, facilitators, venue, safeguards, administration and product improvement.
## Funnel to measure
**Free Sunday attendee → first interactive trial → $5 worksheet / return visit → paid membership → retained member → referral.**
Track separately:
- weekly attendees;
- unique free attendees;
- active paid memberships;
- active participants covered by family memberships;
- free-to-paid conversion within 4 and 8 weeks;
- worksheet conversion;
- membership retention;
- blended revenue per paid membership.
Do not report every participant in a family bundle as a separate paying membership.
