# MC-SAF-002 — Scope of Practice & Distress Escalation Protocol

> Source: Notion Document Hub (controlled copy). Category: Safeguarding. Status: —.
> Regenerate with `node scripts/fetch-controlled-docs.mjs`.

> **Mindcast facilitators refer. They do not diagnose, counsel or treat.**
## Purpose
This protocol defines the line between facilitated personal development and clinical care, and the escalation path when a member becomes distressed or discloses risk.
## Scope of practice
A Mindcast facilitator may:
- guide the published session structure;
- ask open reflective questions;
- normalise passing or taking a break;
- listen without diagnosing;
- help a person identify an appropriate support service;
- escalate safeguarding or immediate-safety concerns.
A facilitator must not:
- diagnose a mental-health condition;
- provide therapy, counselling or treatment;
- tell a member what trauma or symptom 'means';
- advise changing medication or medical treatment;
- promise confidentiality where safety is at risk;
- create a private dependency relationship;
- present themselves as a healer, clinician or authority.
## Distress during a session
1. Keep the room calm and preserve the person's dignity.
1. Offer the person the choice to pass, step outside or sit in the quiet space.
1. A second safety-checked worker checks on them where practicable; do not abandon the wider room.
1. Ask only what is necessary to understand immediate safety, not to investigate their history.
1. If there is immediate danger, call **111**.
1. If there is a child-protection concern, follow MC-SAF-001 immediately.
1. Where appropriate, provide referral information such as 1737 and relevant local services.
1. Record the incident/concern through the approved restricted process before the end of the required reporting window.
## Self-harm or suicide risk
If a person says they intend to harm themselves or someone else, has a plan or means, cannot stay safe, or presents an immediate serious risk, treat it as an urgent safety matter. Do not attempt to become their counsellor. Stay with the immediate safety need, involve emergency/clinical support, and escalate internally after the emergency response has begun.
## After the event
- Record facts separately from opinion.
- Notify the Safeguarding Lead where the concern is safeguarding-related.
- Debrief staff without disclosing unnecessary personal detail.
- Do not discuss the member's story outside the authorised response team.
- Review whether the session content, staffing or referral system needs to change.
## Training gate
Facilitators must pass the scope-of-practice and distress-escalation training module before member-facing duties and repeat it annually.
