# Mindcast — Image Generation Guidelines

For briefing Lovable (or any image model) so generated photography matches the
brand and the real experience. Mindcast is a **premium, produced live learning
event** — closer to a TEDx / modern conference / high-production Sunday gathering
in a real theatre — **not** a wellness retreat, therapy group, or spiritual circle.

## The vibe in one line
Bright, modern, warm, professional, real. Big-room production value, mixed-age
families, workbooks and screens — energy of learning together, not introspection
in the dark.

## Never generate (hard exclusions)
- Candles, tea lights, dim/mood lighting, fire, incense, crystals.
- Circles of people on the floor, cushions, yoga mats, hands-on-heart, meditation
  poses, group hand-holding, eyes-closed "spiritual" moments.
- Women-only groups (crowds must be mixed gender and mixed age).
- Robes, loungewear, boho/hippie styling, flowing scarves.
- Religious iconography, altars, churches with pews / stained glass.
- Stocky corporate clip-art, fake grins to camera, watermarks, text artifacts.

## Settings (three parallel tracks, same building, same time)
- **Adults** — a real tiered **lecture theatre / auditorium**, ~100 adults seated
  in rows facing a large bright presentation **screen**, workbooks open on laps or
  fold-down desks, pens in hand, engaged and taking notes. Stage lighting, pro AV.
- **Children** — a large bright **hall** with round tables, kids doing **colouring
  sheets** and activity workbooks, a couple of facilitators helping. Playful but
  orderly, natural daylight.
- **Teens** — modern **conference / breakout rooms**, teens seated around tables in
  small groups with workbooks, discussion energy, glass walls, daylight.
- **Foyer** — an open lobby with a **coffee station**, mixed-age families mingling,
  warm and social.

## Signature moment (use often)
Members walking through the **theatre doors** and **tapping in with a Mindcast NFC
wristband** — wrist raised to a small reader/turnstile at the entrance, band
visible (brand blue `#3585af`). Both close-ups of the tap and wide shots of people
filing in wearing the bands.

## People & wardrobe
- Adults ages **~35–60**, **smart casual** (chinos, knit, shirt, smart top, nice
  denim) — not suits, not gym gear. Families arriving together (parents + kids + teens).
- Genuinely diverse: ethnicity, gender, body type. Candid, natural, mid-action —
  listening, writing, talking, laughing softly — never posing for camera.

## Look & treatment
- Cinematic, premium, editorial. Bright and clean with warmth (not clinical, not
  moody). Natural + stage light. Subtle analog film **grain** overlay.
- Colour world: brand blue `#3585af`, dark navy `#0a1120`, warm ivory/cream
  `#fffaf6`. Screens / signage / lanyards / wristbands can carry Mindcast blue and
  the logo.
- Mix wide establishing shots with intimate close-ups (a hand writing in a
  workbook, a wristband tap, a child colouring, coffee being poured).
- On-screen/signage text may show the logo tagline **"TUNE INTO YOUR INNER SELF"**
  or the philosophy **"NOTICE IT, NAME IT, DO IT"** — but most image models garble
  text, so treat legible on-image text as optional.

## Negative prompt (for tools that accept one)
```
candles, dim lighting, incense, crystals, yoga mats, meditation circle, hands on
heart, women-only group, robes, boho, religious altar, church pews, corporate
stock, text watermark, extra fingers, distorted hands
```

## Shot list (generate these)
1. **Wide** — ~100 smart-casual adults 35–60 seated in a tiered lecture theatre
   facing a big bright screen, workbooks open, taking notes. Cinematic, warm, grain.
2. **Close-up** — an adult's hands writing in an open Mindcast workbook on a
   fold-down theatre desk, screen glow behind, shallow depth of field.
3. **Kids** — a bright hall, round tables, children doing Mindcast colouring sheets,
   a facilitator kneeling to help, natural daylight, cheerful and orderly.
4. **Teens** — seated around a table in a modern glass-walled conference room,
   workbooks open, mid-discussion, daylight.
5. **Signature tap** — a person raising their wrist with a blue Mindcast NFC
   wristband to a reader at the theatre entrance doors, others filing in behind.
6. **Foyer** — warm lobby coffee station, mixed-age families mingling before doors
   open, wristbands on wrists, Mindcast signage in brand blue.
7. **Family arriving** — parents, a teen and a child walking toward the theatre
   entrance, smart casual, bands on wrists, anticipation.
