## SLIDE 1 — Cover

**Mindcast** — Safe, secular community connection + a 52-week personal-growth curriculum,
delivered live in Taupō and licensed to other cities via software.
Founder: Ashleigh Carlson | Taupō, New Zealand | mindcast.co.nz
Two investable propositions: **(A)** curriculum + app licensing business, **(B)** purpose-built community venue (~$3M NZD).

## SLIDE 2 — Problem

Loneliness and disconnection are rising; existing options (church, therapy, apps) each exclude
large groups. Mindcast is "not therapy, not medicine, not a church" — structured weekly belonging
with safeguarding built in (evidence: `legal/01`, `legal/03`).

## SLIDE 3 — Solution

Live Sunday gatherings + Life Groups, 52-week × 3-track curriculum (adult/teen/kids), member app,
and a purpose-built venue proposition. Curriculum fully built and audited (`08_launch_readiness.md`,
`legal/05_ip_review_52_weeks.md`).

## SLIDE 4 — Market

- Local (Taupō): validated demand table across 8+ segment types; 30+ recurring weekly bookings
  projected within 12 months (`investor_pitch.md` §5).
- Global: licensing model — curriculum + software licensed to facilitators in other cities
  (`09_franchise_architecture.md`). Multi-track content is language/format portable.

## SLIDE 5 — Business model

Membership tiers (free on-ramp → paid → concession), workbooks, sponsorship/koha, licensing from
Year 2+ (`02_financial_forecast.md` §4–5). Venue: dual-use rental model, $528k–$758k projected
annual revenue (`investor_pitch.md` §3).

## SLIDE 6 — Traction & milestones

Founding-member NFC cohort (first 100 bracelets purchased), curriculum complete, app in build
(`12_todo_gap_matrix.md`), operations runbook written (`OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md`).
*Next milestone: pilot term with paying members — the traction proof every funder asks for.*

## SLIDE 7 — Financial projections

- Community business: phased cost model, break-even path at 124 committed members
  (`02_financial_forecast.md`).
- Venue: $2.6M–$3.45M build; 65% occupancy = $323k net operating income, 10.8% ROI, cash-flow
  positive year one (`investor_pitch.md` §6).

## SLIDE 8 — The ask

- Venue: $3M via debt, equity (30–49% for $1.5–3M), or hybrid (`investor_pitch.md` §8–9).
- Community/licensing arm: milestone-based working capital (Kiwibank StartUp+ pathway, up to $150k)
  + council community grants; seed equity round deferred until entity + private lead in place
  (`funding_eligibility_verdicts.md`).

## SLIDE 9 — Team & governance

Founder: Ashleigh Carlson (founder + brand strategist). Advisory board/governance group planned
from day one for safeguarding credibility (`01_vision_and_strategy.md`, `legal/03`).
⚠️ Board not yet constituted — close before institutional capital.

## SLIDE 10 — Risk & safeguarding (the differentiator)

Anti-cult charter, vetting, moderation, privacy (NZ Privacy Act 2020), IP audit completed
(`legal/03`, `legal/04`, `legal/05`). This is the evidence pack most community ventures lack.
