# 01 · Vision & Strategy

*Written from the perspective of a founder + brand strategist.*

---

## Vision statement
> **A world where every neighbourhood has a room you can walk into and become
> more yourself — together.**

Mindcast exists so that ordinary people, in real rooms, can do the inner work of
understanding themselves and quietly change their lives — supported by a
community that knows their name.

## Purpose (mission)
> **We turn timeless wisdom into a weekly practice people actually keep —
> face-to-face, across all ages — so insight becomes a lived, shared change.**

Most self-development is consumed alone and forgotten by Thursday. Mindcast fixes
the *implementation* gap: a facilitated 52-week journey, a live Sunday gathering,
a private reflection, and a midweek Life Group — for adults, teens and children,
side by side.

## Objectives (the next 12 months)
1. Run a **sustainable weekly gathering in Taupō** that covers its own costs.
2. Reach a **membership base that lets us pay facilitators** (not rely on
   volunteers long-term).
3. Ship the **app + hardcopy workbook** so members can journal and go deeper.
4. Prove one full **13-week block** (a "phase") end-to-end, then the full year.
5. Document everything so it can be **licensed to a second town** in Year 2–3.

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)
> "You know how everyone listens to podcasts and reads the self-help books, but
> nothing actually changes? Mindcast is a weekly, in-person gathering — think a
> gym membership, but for your inner life. Every Sunday a facilitator guides one
> theme on the big screen; you reflect in your own private journal, then go
> deeper midweek with a small group who know your name. Adults, teens and kids
> each have their own track, so whole families grow together. It's a 52-week
> journey, it runs here in Taupō, and it's built so a real community — not an
> app — is the thing that changes your life."

**One-liner:** *A gym membership for your inner life — done together, in person.*

| Passive (listen, scroll) | Facilitated + interactive on a big screen |

## Business model
Mindcast is a **membership community** with light product and licensing revenue.
Value flows from a weekly live experience; money follows only enough to sustain
it.

**Revenue streams (in priority order):**
1. **Recurring memberships** — adult, teen, and a kids add-on (the engine).
2. **Free "Community" tier** — attend the live session, no app; buy the
   **hardcopy workbook** or a **single-session handout** to participate at home.
3. **Products** — printed workbooks, handouts, and eventually branded NFC
   bracelets beyond the first 100.
4. **Koha / sponsorship** — local businesses sponsoring a term or a family's
   membership (very on-brand in NZ).
5. **Licensing (Year 2+)** — the lessons + software licensed to facilitators in
   other towns. This is the long-term scale play, kept separate from the local
   community (see structure below).

**Cost drivers:** hall hire ($1,000/week), facilitator pay, insurance,
software/infra, printing. Detailed in [02](02_financial_forecast.md).

**Why memberships fund it:** a small, committed recurring base is more stable
than ticket sales or ad revenue, and it matches the mission — people who *belong*,
not customers who *buy*.

## Values (the operating principles)
- **Community over content.** The room matters more than the material.
- **Draw out, don't preach.** We surface each person's own inner wisdom; we never
  impose a belief system or a leader's authority.
- **Everyone can afford to belong.** Concession / pay-what-you-can protects
  access; no one is turned away for money.
- **Safe by design.** Vetting, moderation and safeguarding are not optional
  extras — they're the product (see [L3](legal/03_safeguarding_ethics_anticult.md)).
- **Honest about what we are.** We're not therapy, not medicine, not a church.
  We say so, clearly (see [L1](legal/01_disclaimer_and_participation_terms.md)).

## Legal structure — the biggest open decision
Your goals point two directions at once: a **not-for-profit community** locally,
and a **for-profit licensing** business nationally/globally. NZ options:

| Option | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sole trader** | Fastest to start | You are personally liable — risky given you work with children and vulnerable people. Fine for month 1, not for long. |
| **Company (Ltd)** | Good for licensing | Limited liability; clean for IP + licensing revenue; taxed as a business. |
| **Charitable Trust / Incorporated Society** | Best for the community mission | Access to grants + koha, strong "not a cult / for-the-people" signal, but restricts private profit and licensing. |
| **Hybrid (recommended to explore)** | Best of both | A **charitable trust or incorporated society** runs the *local community* (grant-eligible, mission-locked, anti-cult optics) **+** a separate **Ltd company** that owns the *IP and does the licensing*, and licenses to the trust for $0/nominal. |

**Recommendation:** start as a **sole trader for the first pilot term only**
while cash is zero, and in parallel get 30 minutes with a NZ community-law
solicitor and an accountant to set up the **hybrid** before you take real
membership money or hire anyone. The structure affects tax, liability,
safeguarding obligations, and grant eligibility — do it early.
