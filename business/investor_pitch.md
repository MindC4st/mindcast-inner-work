# Mindcast Venue — Investor Pitch

**Project:** Purpose-built community venue, Taupō, New Zealand  
**Capital required:** ~$3M NZD  
**Structure:** Debt or equity partnership | land + build  
**Target return:** 8–12% p.a. | 5–7 year exit pathway  

---

## 1. THE PROBLEM

Churches across New Zealand sit empty six days a week — millions of dollars in real estate serving one morning. Meanwhile, secular community groups (yoga studios, co-working collectives, parent groups, men's circles, grief support, AA, book clubs, dance schools) scramble for affordable, well-equipped rooms. They rent school halls with bad acoustics, community centres with fluorescent lighting, or cafes that close at 3pm.

There is no building in New Zealand purpose-built for *secular weekly gathering* — a room designed to hold accountability, reflection, and real conversation.

---

## 2. THE SOLUTION

A 600–800m² commercial building in Taupō designed around three revenue-generating functions:

| Space | Capacity | Features |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Theatre / Auditorium** | 120 seats | Raked seating, LED wall, stage lighting, acoustic treatment, blackout capability |
| **Breakout rooms × 6** | 15 each | Frameless glass walls, sliding doors, wall-mounted displays, acoustic panels |
| **Cafe** | 30 seated | Espresso bar, communal tables, servery, opens to forecourt |
| **Indoor playground** | 15–20 kids | Soft-fall flooring, climbing structure, visible through glass from cafe |
| **Reception / lounge** | — | Welcome desk, coat storage, lockable cupboards per room |

The design is deliberately flexible: every room is bookable independently during the day, then the full building operates as one integrated venue on Sunday mornings and Tuesday evenings.

## 4. THE SECRET WEAPON: LOCKABLE CUPBOARDS

Each breakout room has a built-in lockable cupboard (1.2m wide × 2.1m high). External groups that book recurring slots store their gear on-site — yoga mats, circle cushions, whiteboard kits, A/V equipment. This is sticky infrastructure:

- Reduces setup/teardown friction to near-zero
- Creates switching costs that retain tenants
- Enables higher hourly rates (premium for storage)
- Means Tuesday Life Groups walk into clean, clear, ready rooms

The cupboards are the moat.

## 7. WHY THIS WORKS

**Dual-use efficiency.** The building earns during the day from external groups and serves its purpose at night. No dead hours. No single-use asset.

**Anchor tenant de-risks the model.** Mindcast covers base costs regardless of external occupancy. External revenue is upside.

**Lock-in through infrastructure.** Lockable cupboards create tenant stickiness. Groups stay because leaving means finding new storage, new setup, new logistics.

**Taupō is growing.** Population grew 12% between 2018–2023. Remote workers are relocating. Community infrastructure has not kept pace.

**No direct competitor.** There is no secular, multi-room, purpose-built gathering venue in regional New Zealand. Churches own this category by default. This building competes on design, not doctrine.

## 8. INVESTMENT STRUCTURE (OPTIONS)

### Option A: Debt (mortgage)

- $3M loan at 6.5–7.5% commercial rate
- Annual interest: $195,000–$225,000
- Covered by external rental income alone at 65% occupancy
- Full ownership retained

### Option B: Equity partnership

- Investor contributes $1.5–3M for 30–49% equity
- Returns paid as dividends from operating profit
- 5–7 year exit via buyback or sale
- Ideal for impact investors / values-aligned capital

### Option C: Hybrid

- $1.5M equity + $1.5M bank debt
- Lower debt service → faster profitability
- Equity partner gets board seat + quarterly reporting
