# 02 · Financial Forecast

*Written from the perspective of a small-business accountant / CFO. All figures
NZD, GST-exclusive unless noted. These are planning estimates — confirm with a
NZ accountant, especially GST registration (mandatory once turnover > $60k/yr).*

---

## 1. The core cost problem, stated plainly
The hall is the beast: **$1,000/week = ~$4,333/month = $52,000/year.** Everything
else is small by comparison. So the whole financial model is really one question:

> **How many members, at what price, cover the hall — and then the people?**

## 2. Cost model (three phases)

### Phase 1 — Bootstrap / survival (months 0–6)
Goal: cover the hall + basics. You (founder) unpaid, minimal volunteers.

| Cost | Monthly | Annual | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Hall hire | $4,333 | $52,000 | The number that must be beaten |
| Software / infra | $70 | $840 | Supabase Pro ($25) + Vercel Pro ($20) + domain/email + buffer |
| Insurance (public liability + prof. indemnity) | $150 | $1,800 | **Essential** — do not run a session uninsured |
| Printing / materials | $125 | $1,500 | Posters, handouts, workbook proofs |
| Accounting / compliance | $125 | $1,500 | Annual returns, GST, advice |
| Payment fees (Stripe) | ~2.9% + 30c/txn | — | Modelled against revenue |
| **Total (excl. Stripe)** | **~$4,803** | **~$57,640** | |

### Phase 2 — Paying facilitators (months 6–18)
Goal: stop relying on volunteers for delivery. Add paid facilitation.

| Added cost | Monthly | Annual | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Lead facilitator | $1,040 | $12,480 | ~$40/hr × 6 hrs/wk (prep + deliver) |
| Kids/teen helper (paid) | $520 | $6,240 | ~$30/hr × 4 hrs/wk |
| **Phase 2 total (with Phase 1)** | **~$6,363** | **~$76,360** | |

### Phase 3 — Sustainable (18 months+)
Adds a part-time coordinator/admin, reserves, and a second facilitator as tracks
grow. Budget **~$8,000–9,000/month (~$96k–108k/yr)**. This is the "healthy,
resilient, not-founder-dependent" state.

## 3. Break-even: members needed
Assumes ~3% payment fees, so a $40 plan nets ~$38.80.

| Adult price / mo | Net / mo | Members to cover **hall only** ($4,333) | To cover **Phase 1** ($4,803) | To cover **Phase 2** ($6,363) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $30 | $29.10 | 149 | 165 | 219 |
| $35 | $33.95 | 128 | 142 | 188 |
| **$40** | **$38.80** | **112** | **124** | **164** |
| $45 | $43.65 | 100 | 110 | 146 |
| $50 | $48.50 | 90 | 99 | 132 |

**Read this as your dashboard.** At **$40/month**, ~**124 paying adults** covers
the whole bootstrap operation; ~**164** lets you pay facilitators properly.
Workbook/handout sales and koha push those numbers down.

> Reality check: 124 committed members is a genuine community, not a quick win.
> Expect to reach it over 2–4 terms, using the free tier and product sales to
> bridge the gap while the paid base grows.

## 4. Recommended pricing tiers (NZD)
Designed so the free tier is a genuine on-ramp, the paid tier funds the room, and
no one is excluded.

| Tier | Price | What they get |
|---|---:|---|
| **Community (Free)** | $0 | Attend the live Sunday session. **No app**, so no on-screen contribution, no journal. Can **buy the workbook or a single-session handout** to participate at home. |
| **Adult** | **$40/mo** or **$110/term** (13 wks) | Full app: current-week lesson, video, private journal, on-screen contribution, Life Group. |
| **Teen (13–17)** | **$20/mo** | Teen track only, full app. |
| **Kids add-on** | **+$15/mo** | Adds children's lessons + downloadable colouring pages to a paying adult's account (kids don't log in). |
| **Family** | **$75/mo** | Two adults + kids add-on, one household. |
| **Founding 100** | **$30/mo locked for life** + **free NFC bracelet** | First 100 members. Rewards early belief, seeds the base, creates scarcity. |
| **Concession / Koha** | Pay-what-you-can | Hardship or by arrangement. Protects the "everyone belongs" value. |

**Products (one-off):**
- Hardcopy workbook (full 13-week block): **$35** (print cost ~$10–12 → margin funds bracelets/printing).
- Single-session handout: **$5**.
- NFC bracelet (after the free 100): **$15**.

## 5. Supplementary & bridge revenue
- **Workbook/handout sales** to free members — your best bridge while the paid
  base is small. 40 workbooks/term ≈ $1,000 margin.
- **Local sponsorship** — a café or business sponsors a term (~$500–1,000) for a
  poster credit. Very achievable in Taupō.
- **Grants** — if you set up the charitable/incorporated arm (see [01](01_vision_and_strategy.md)),
  NZ community grants (e.g. COGS, local council, Lotteries) can offset hall hire.
- **The first 100 NFC bracelets** are already bought — treat them as a
  **founding-member acquisition tool**, not a cost.

## 6. First-year illustrative scenario (conservative)
Assumes a slow, honest ramp; founder unpaid; free tier + products bridging.

| Quarter | Paying adults | Free members | Monthly membership rev | Position |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Q1 (launch term) | 25 | 40 | ~$1,000 | Deep loss — cover hall via a cheaper/subsidised venue + workbook sales + koha |
| Q2 | 55 | 60 | ~$2,200 | Still subsidised; sponsorship + grants critical |
| Q3 | 90 | 80 | ~$3,600 | Near hall break-even |
| Q4 | 125 | 100 | ~$5,000 | Hall + Phase-1 covered; begin paying facilitator |

**Key insight:** in the early quarters the **hall is unaffordable at full price**.
Bootstrap tactics for months 0–6:
- Negotiate a **community/off-peak hall rate**, a church hall, or a school hall
  (often far below $1,000).
- **Start smaller** — one track, fewer weeks, cheaper room — and grow into the
  $1,000 venue once the base supports it.
- Use **grants + sponsorship** explicitly to cover the venue gap while members
  grow.

## 7. Decisions to confirm
- **GST:** register now or at the $60k threshold? (Affects pricing display.)
- **Hall:** can you secure a cheaper venue for the first 2 terms? This single
  decision changes the whole runway.
- Term length for pricing: 13-week blocks (matches the 4 phases) — confirm.
- Founding-member cap: 100 (matches your bracelets) — confirm the locked rate.
- Do you want an annual pre-pay option (e.g. 2 months free) to pull cash forward?
