# Curriculum — All 52 Sessions, Themes & Relevance to Modern Living

*A full map of the 52-week journey so you can see the shape at a glance and judge
which sessions land hardest for how people actually live today (digital
overwhelm, comparison, anxiety, burnout, loneliness, meaning).*

**Relevance key (to *today's* living):**
⭐⭐⭐ = speaks directly to a defining modern struggle ·
⭐⭐ = strongly relevant ·

## Most relevant to today's living (the ⭐⭐⭐ shortlist)
If you ever wanted a "greatest hits" arc, a taster series, or the sessions to lead
your marketing with, these hit the hardest modern nerves:

1. **The Comparison Loop** (6) — the social-media wound, named.
2. **The Inner Critic** (7) — the anxiety/self-criticism epidemic.
3. **The Signal and the Noise** (1) — attention + overwhelm.
4. **Rewiring Fear — Burnt Toast, Not Fire** (24) — anxiety, reframed.
5. **Breaking the People-Pleasing Pattern / Boundaries** (19) — the boundaries era.
6. **The People You Choose** (31) — the loneliness epidemic.
7. **Rest as a Practice, Not a Reward** (34) — burnout culture.
8. **The Masks We Wear** (10) — authenticity vs. the online persona.
9. **Purpose — What You're Here For** (36) — the meaning/"quiet quitting" crisis.
10. **Enough — Living From Sufficiency** (46) — consumerism / never-enough.
11. **Conflict as a Growth Practice** (44) — communication in a polarised world.
12. **How You Speak to Yourself** (32) — the inner voice that runs everything.

**The pattern:** the arc's centre of gravity for *modern* life is the cluster
around **attention, comparison, the inner critic, boundaries, loneliness, rest and
meaning.** Almost none of the 52 feel dated — the only "low relevance" marks are
the 4 integration weeks, which are structural bookends, not weak content.
