# 05 · HR & Safeguarding Plan

*Written from the perspective of an HR + safeguarding lead. Because Mindcast works
with children, teens and emotionally vulnerable adults, safeguarding is not a
back-office task — it is central to the product and to protecting you. Have this
reviewed by a NZ employment lawyer + your insurer.*

---

## The people journey (volunteers → paid, safely)
You start as a one-person band. The path off that:

```
Founder (you)  →  Vetted volunteers (core team)  →  Paid facilitators & staff
   month 0            months 1–6                        months 6–18+
```

The rule that never bends: **anyone in a role of trust — especially with under-18s
or vulnerable people — is vetted and trained BEFORE they start.** No exceptions,
including friends who are "obviously fine."

---

## 1. Recruiting like-minded people (the early volunteer core)
You need 3–6 volunteers first: setup/AV, welcome/host, and a children's/teen
helper. Where to find aligned people:
- **Your founding members** — the people already drawn to the mission are your
  best volunteers.
- **Local community boards, churches/marae, service clubs, uni students** (social
  work / psychology / education students value the experience).
- **Volunteer NZ / SEEK Volunteer / local volunteer centres.**
- A clear **"Volunteer with Mindcast" one-pager** describing the mission, the
  values, the vetting (people who want to help *want* to see you take safety
  seriously — it's a trust signal, not a barrier).

**Screen for values, not just availability:** humility, warmth, discretion, no
"guru" energy. One charismatic-but-controlling volunteer can undo the whole
anti-cult culture (see [L3](legal/03_safeguarding_ethics_anticult.md)).

## 2. Vetting (the non-negotiable gate)
Everyone in a trust role completes, **before their first session**:
- **NZ Police vetting** via the **NZ Police Vetting Service** (free for approved
  organisations; you register as an organisation). Re-vet every **3 years**.
- **Children's worker safety check** under the **Children's Act 2014** for anyone
  working with the kids/teen tracks — this is a legal requirement, not optional
  (identity, Police vet, referee checks, risk assessment, periodic re-check).
- **Two reference checks.**
- **Signed** Code of Conduct + Safeguarding Policy + Confidentiality agreement.

Keep a simple **vetting register** (who, role, vet date, expiry). The app's
household/roles system can track staff roles; keep the sensitive vetting records
in a separate, access-controlled place.

## 3. From volunteer to paid staff
When [02](02_financial_forecast.md) shows the base can afford it (~160+ paying
adults), convert your best volunteers to paid facilitators:
- Start with **one paid lead facilitator**, then a **paid kids/teen helper**.
- Pay at or above a fair local rate — paying people is a *values* statement
  ("we don't run on unpaid labour forever") and a quality/reliability lever.
- Use proper **employment agreements** (below). Even casual/contractor roles need
  written terms in NZ.

## 4. Contracts & documents you need
Get these drafted/reviewed by a NZ employment lawyer:
1. **Volunteer Agreement** — role, expectations, values, confidentiality,
   safeguarding, no-employment-relationship clause, image/consent.
2. **Employment Agreement** (when paying) — NZ-compliant (Employment Relations
   Act): role, hours, pay, holidays, 90-day/trial rules if used, code of conduct,
   IP assignment (so lesson tweaks belong to Mindcast), confidentiality.
3. **Contractor Agreement** (if using contractors) — clear it's not employment;
   IP assignment; insurance expectations.
4. **Code of Conduct** — behaviour, boundaries, no dual relationships, no
   financial dealings with members, mandatory reporting.
5. **Confidentiality & Privacy Agreement** — members' journals and shares are
   private; breaching it is a dismissable offence.
6. **Safeguarding Policy acknowledgement** (see [L3](legal/03_safeguarding_ethics_anticult.md)).

## 5. Onboarding checklist (every new team member)
- [ ] Application + values conversation
- [ ] Police vet / children's-worker check **cleared**
- [ ] References checked
- [ ] Agreement + Code of Conduct + Confidentiality **signed**
- [ ] Safeguarding + privacy training completed
- [ ] Shadowed **2 sessions** before leading anything
- [ ] Added to roster + given only the app access their role needs (facilitator
      vs admin — least privilege)
- [ ] Emergency procedures + first-aid/venue safety briefed

## 6. Training manual (outline to build)
A simple manual (start as a Google Doc, grow it):
1. **Mindcast in a nutshell** — vision, values, the no-deity / draw-out-not-preach
   philosophy.
2. **The weekly ritual** — setup, AV, check-in, running the FacilitatorView,
   pace, closing.
3. **Facilitation skills** — holding space, not fixing, not advising, staying in
   "educator" lane (never "therapist"), handling a distressed participant.
4. **Safeguarding** — spotting/reporting concerns, boundaries, mandatory
   reporting, escalation path, when to call for help.
5. **Privacy** — journals are sacred; moderation before on-screen; what never
   leaves the room.
6. **The app** — check-in kiosk, moderation queue, troubleshooting.
7. **Emergency & wellbeing** — first aid, venue evac, a distressed member script,
   local mental-health referral contacts (because you are **not** the
   professional — you refer).

## 7. Wellbeing & referral (protects members AND you)
- Keep a **referral card** of local professional services (GP, counsellors,
  helplines: e.g. 1737 "Need to talk?" in NZ). Facilitators refer; they don't
  treat.
- A clear line: *if someone is in crisis, we support and refer — we are not a
  clinical service.* This is both care and legal protection (see [L1](legal/01_disclaimer_and_participation_terms.md)).

## Decisions to confirm
- Register Mindcast as an **approved organisation** for Police vetting (do early —
  it takes time).
- Who is your **Safeguarding Lead** (can be you initially, but name it and, as you
  grow, separate it from the founder for independence).
- First 3 volunteer roles to fill and who you'll approach.
- Draft the Volunteer Agreement first (you'll need it before tasters).
