# 03 · Business Plan — Pre-launch, Launch & Marketing

*Written from the perspective of a community-led growth strategist. The whole
plan assumes near-zero ad budget and a bootstrapped, in-person-first product.*

---

## The core growth truth
Mindcast is a **local, in-person, trust-based** product. That changes everything
about marketing: your growth engine is **word of mouth + physical presence +
belonging**, not digital ads. People join because a friend brought them, or
because they saw something real in their town — not because an algorithm served
them a video.

> **Modern reality check:** paid social (especially Facebook/Instagram feed ads)
> is a weak channel for the 30+ audience — many have drifted off or tuned out,
> and cost-per-trust is high. Your instinct about **paper posters in cafés is
> correct** and on-strategy: for a local, human product, physical + relational
> channels beat digital ads. Use digital to *support* real-world discovery, not
> replace it.

---

## Phase A — Pre-launch (weeks −8 to 0)

**Goal:** line up the room, the tech, the safety, and the first believers so
launch night is full and safe.

### Foundations (do first)
- [ ] Lock the **legal structure** + **insurance** (see [01](01_vision_and_strategy.md)/[02](02_financial_forecast.md)).
- [ ] Secure the **venue** (negotiate a community rate — see [02](02_financial_forecast.md) §6).
- [ ] Finish the **tech must-haves** (Stripe live, own Supabase/Vercel, moderation) — see [04](04_technical_plan.md).
- [ ] Draft + get reviewed: **disclaimer, safeguarding policy, privacy policy** (`legal/`).
- [ ] Set the **program start date** (first Sunday) in `/admin/program`.
- [ ] Print: **posters, single-session handout, workbook proof, founding-member cards**.

### Build the first believers (your "1,000 true fans" but you only need ~30)
- [ ] Personally invite **20–30 people you know** would value this — one-to-one,
      not a broadcast. These become your **founding core**.
- [ ] Run **2–3 free "taster" sessions** (a single powerful lesson) to let people
      feel the room before committing.
- [ ] Recruit **3–5 volunteers** for setup/welcome/AV (see [05](05_hr_and_safeguarding.md)).
- [ ] Start a simple **email/WhatsApp list** — the one digital asset you own and
      control (never rely on a social platform you don't own).

### The founding-member offer (your launch hook)
- **First 100 members** get the **founding rate ($30/mo locked for life)** + a
  **free NFC bracelet**. Scarcity + reward + belonging in one offer.
- Frame it as *"help us build this"* not *"buy this"* — founders want to belong to
  something they helped start.

## Phase B — Launch (the first term, 13 weeks)

**Goal:** a full, safe, repeatable weekly gathering that people talk about.

- [ ] **Launch night** = your taster crowd + their +1s. Aim for a warm, full room
      over a big empty one.
- [ ] Nail the **weekly ritual**: arrive → NFC check-in → name on the welcome wall
      → facilitated session → private reflection → invite to the Life Group. The
      ritual *is* the product.
- [ ] **Every member brings one person** by week 4 — make this an explicit, gentle
      ask. Referral is your #1 channel.
- [ ] Capture **testimonials/quotes** (with consent) from week 2 onward — these
      become your marketing.
- [ ] Sell **workbooks + handouts** at the door from night one (bridge revenue +
      lets free members participate).
- [ ] Weekly **debrief + tweak** — treat term 1 as a live prototype.

## Phase C — Marketing & growth (ongoing)

### Channel priority (highest trust-per-dollar first)
1. **Word of mouth / referral** — the "bring a friend" ritual, founding-member
   advocacy, a simple "gift a session" card.
2. **Physical presence** — **posters in cafés, libraries, gyms, health-food
   shops, community boards, school newsletters**. Beautiful, minimal, a QR code
   to the site. This is your café-poster instinct, systematised. Rotate designs;
   ask café owners directly (many will say yes to a local wellbeing thing).
3. **Local partnerships** — GPs/social workers/counsellors who can *refer* (be
   careful to stay "not therapy" — see [L1](legal/01_disclaimer_and_participation_terms.md)),
   schools (teen track), churches/marae/community groups, employers (staff
   wellbeing).
4. **Earned local media** — Taupō community paper, local radio, community FB
   *groups* (not ads — genuine posts in local groups perform far better than paid
   feed ads for this audience).
5. **Owned digital** — a clean website, an email list, and short *authentic*
   video/reels of the room (organic, not paid). Content that shows real people in
   a real room converts; polished ads don't.
6. **Paid ads — last, and only geo-targeted + small.** If used at all: a tiny
   local radius, retargeting website visitors, or Google search for
   "[town] wellbeing group". Do **not** pour money into feed ads.

### The NFC bracelets as marketing
Your 100 bracelets are a **walking billboard + belonging token**. A member wearing
a Mindcast bracelet at the café *is* the poster. Founding members get them free;
after that they're a $15 product and a conversation-starter.

### Content that actually works today (2020s, 30+ audience)
- **Real over polished** — a shaky 20-second clip of the room's energy beats a
  glossy ad.
- **Founder story** — people back people. Your passion + "one-man band building
  this for the community" is the most compelling asset you have. Use it.
- **Micro-proof** — one member's honest sentence ("I finally kept a promise to
  myself") outperforms any slogan.
- **Local, specific, human** — name the town, show the faces (with consent), tell
  the truth.

## 90-day launch checklist (condensed)
**Days 1–30 (pre-launch):** structure + insurance + venue + tech + legal +
posters printed + 30 personal invites + taster sessions booked.
**Days 31–60 (soft launch):** run tasters, recruit volunteers, open founding-100
signups, seed café posters, start email list.
**Days 61–90 (launch term begins):** weekly gathering live, "bring a friend"
ritual, door sales, collect testimonials, weekly debriefs, first sponsorship
conversations.

## Decisions to confirm
- Launch date + first venue.
- Founding-100 offer wording + how you'll fulfil the free bracelets.
- Which 3 local partners to approach first (café / school / GP).
- Who owns marketing in month 1 (likely you) and who you'll delegate it to.
