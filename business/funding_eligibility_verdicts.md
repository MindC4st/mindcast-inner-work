## 1. Kiwibank StartUp+

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Business **debt** (loan), not a grant. Up to **$150,000 NZD** in milestone-based tranches; no requirement for 2 years' accounts or collateral — assessed on milestones + growth potential. |
| How it's accessed | Delivered in partnership with **Ministry of Awesome** to startups in the **Founder Catalyst programme**. Founder Catalyst Summer 2027 cohort: **EOI due 1 November 2026** (ministryofawesome.com/eoi-one). |
| Standard conditions | Kiwibank eligibility, lending criteria, T&Cs and fees still apply (it is a loan; must be serviceable). |

**Verdict: ELIGIBLE TO PURSUE — but only via Founder Catalyst, and only for proposition (A) working capital.**

- ✅ Fits the design intent: early-stage, no trading history, milestone-based.
- ✅ Founder Catalyst is explicitly for founders raising capital and building toward scale.
- ⚠️ Path: submit Founder Catalyst EOI by **1 Nov 2026** → accepted into cohort → Kiwibank StartUp+ becomes available.
- ⚠️ Requires a registered NZ business entity + credible cash-flow forecast showing loan serviceability (`02_financial_forecast.md` Phase 2/3 numbers are the evidence base).
- ❌ Not a fit for the $3M venue build — cap is $150k and it's working-capital debt, not property finance.

**Action:** EOI for Founder Catalyst before 1 Nov 2026; register the entity first.

| Item | Detail (business.govt.nz, reviewed 29 Jul 2026) |
|---|---|
| What it is | Crown-backed **equity co-investment**, $50,000–$1.5M per round, ongoing/rolling. |
| Hard criteria | (1) **Technology startup**; (2) seeking investment **in exchange for equity**; (3) **private co-investment must already be secured** alongside NZGCP. Competitive, case-by-case. |

**Verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE TODAY — conditional path exists for the licensing/software arm only.**

- ✅ Company now exists (Mindcast Limited, 9416927) and can issue shares — but no cap table, no shareholders' agreement yet.
- ❌ No private lead investor secured (a prerequisite, not a nice-to-have).
- ⚠️ "Technology startup" test: Mindcast's community/venue model is not a tech venture. The **app + curriculum licensing** arm (franchise architecture, `09_franchise_architecture.md`) is the only piece that could qualify — and only if positioned as a high-growth, scalable software/IP business in its own Ltd company.
- ✅ If the hybrid structure in `01_vision_and_strategy.md` is executed (Ltd owns IP/software), a private lead is found, and an equity round is raised, Aspire can co-invest $50k–$1.5M.

**Action:** Park until (a) entity formed, (b) private lead committed. Revisit at the licensing-arm seed round.
