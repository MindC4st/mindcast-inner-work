# Legal · Disclaimer & Participation Terms (DRAFT)

> **⚠️ This is a founder draft, not legal advice.** Have a New Zealand lawyer
> review and adapt it before you publish or rely on it. It is designed to protect
> you by being honest about what Mindcast is and isn't.

This should appear (a) on the website (`/terms`), (b) in the member sign-up flow
as a tick-box "I have read and agree", and (c) referenced in the workbook.

---

## 1. What Mindcast is
Mindcast is an **educational and community wellbeing programme**. We facilitate
group sessions and provide reflective materials that collate and reframe ideas
from philosophy, psychology, and wisdom traditions to help people understand
themselves and take small, practical steps toward a life they value.

## 2. What Mindcast is **not** (please read carefully)
- Mindcast, its founder, facilitators and volunteers are **not doctors,
  psychologists, psychotherapists, counsellors, or trained health
  professionals**, and Mindcast is **not** a health, medical, psychological, or
  therapeutic service.
- Nothing in our sessions, app, workbook or materials is **medical, psychological,
  therapeutic, financial, or legal advice**, or a diagnosis or treatment of any
  condition.
- The material is **collated and reframed general information**, shared in good
  faith in the hope it helps people discover more about themselves — it is **not a
  substitute for professional care**.

## 3. Please seek professional help when you need it
If you are experiencing mental or physical health difficulties, distress, or
crisis, please consult a qualified professional. In New Zealand you can call or
text **1737** (free, 24/7) to talk with a trained counsellor, or contact your GP
or emergency services (111) in an emergency. Facilitators may gently **refer** you
to professional support; they will never attempt to treat you.

## 4. Voluntary participation & personal responsibility
- Participation is **entirely voluntary**. You may pause, decline any activity, or
  leave at any time, for any reason, with no penalty or pressure.
- You are responsible for your own decisions and wellbeing. You take part using
  your own judgement and at your own risk, and agree that what you choose to do
  with any idea from Mindcast is your choice and responsibility.
- You agree to engage respectfully and to keep others' shares confidential (what's
  shared in the room stays in the room).

## 5. No guarantees
We share these ideas because we believe in them, but **we make no promises about
outcomes or results.** Everyone is different; personal growth is not guaranteed.

## 6. Limitation of liability
To the maximum extent permitted by New Zealand law, Mindcast and its people are
not liable for any loss, harm or damage arising from your participation or from
your use of the materials, except where liability cannot be excluded by law
(including the Consumer Guarantees Act, where it applies). *[Lawyer to finalise
the exact wording and any consumer-law carve-outs.]*

## 7. Children & young people
Where children or teens take part, a parent/guardian is responsible for their
participation and consents on their behalf. Safeguarding is governed by our
[Safeguarding & Ethics Charter](03_safeguarding_ethics_anticult.md).

## 8. Your agreement
By joining a session, creating an account, or using the app or materials, you
confirm you have read, understood and agree to these terms.

---

### Why this protects you
- It **repeatedly and clearly** states you are not a professional and this is not
  therapy/medicine — the single most important protection against "practising
  without a licence" or "you gave me advice" claims.
- It frames the content as **collated general information shared in good faith** —
  your exact intent.
- It builds in **voluntariness + freedom to leave** (also core to the anti-cult
  protection).
- It **refers** distressed people to real help — care *and* liability defence.

### Decisions for your lawyer
- Exact liability wording vs the Consumer Guarantees Act / Fair Trading Act.
- Whether a stronger signed **waiver/assumption-of-risk** is warranted for the
  in-person sessions (likely yes) in addition to the online tick-box.
- Age of consent for teen self-participation vs guardian consent.
