# Legal · Safeguarding, Ethics & Anti-Cult Charter (DRAFT)

> **⚠️ Founder draft — review with a NZ safeguarding advisor + lawyer + your
> insurer.** This is the document that proves, in writing and in practice, that
> Mindcast is a **healthy, open, voluntary community — not a cult and not a
> personality machine.** Publish it, live it, and point to it whenever anyone
> asks.

The best protection against being *seen* as a cult is to **structurally not be
one** — and to be visibly transparent about it. Cults concentrate power, control
information, exploit money, isolate people, and punish leaving. This charter does
the opposite on every axis.

---

## Our promises to every member (the Anti-Cult Charter)

**1. Participation is voluntary — always.**
You join freely, take part freely, and may leave at any time with no guilt trip,
no penalty, no "consequences," and no chasing. Leaving is treated as normal and
fine.

**2. Question everything.**
Disagreement, doubt and challenge are welcomed, never punished. No idea, teaching,
or person is beyond question — including the founder. There are no secret "higher
levels" and no hidden teachings.

**3. No guru. No worship. No single authority.**
Mindcast draws out *your own* inner wisdom — it does not install a leader's
beliefs. No one is elevated as infallible, "chosen," or specially enlightened.
The founder is a facilitator and organiser, not a spiritual authority.

**4. Your life stays yours.**
We will never ask you to cut off family or friends, hand over major life
decisions, move, give up work, or prioritise Mindcast over your own relationships
and responsibilities. We encourage connection *outside* Mindcast, not isolation
within it.

**5. Money is for costs, not control.**
Fees exist only to cover the room and the people who run it (see the open
[financials](../02_financial_forecast.md)). We will never pressure you for
donations, escalating payments, or "love offerings." Concessions exist so money
never decides who belongs. Our finances are transparent to members.

**6. No high-pressure recruitment.**
We grow by invitation and genuine word of mouth. We don't use manipulation,
love-bombing, fear, shame, or urgency to recruit or retain anyone.

**7. Privacy and dignity.**
What you share is confidential. Nothing you write or say is used against you,
displayed without your consent, or shared outside the room. Public/on-screen
sharing is always **opt-in and moderated**.

**8. Open doors and outside eyes.**
Guests, family, and (where appropriate) media or advisors are welcome to observe.
We keep an **independent advisory group** and a real **complaints process** (see
below). We have nothing to hide.

## Governance (why structure matters)
- **Distributed decision-making.** As soon as practical, form a small **advisory
  board / governance group** (3–5 people, including at least one independent of
  the founder). This prevents founder-centric power and is powerful anti-cult
  optics.
- **Written policies** (this charter, safeguarding, privacy, finances) that anyone
  can read.
- **Regular, honest reporting** to members on money and decisions.

## Safeguarding (children, teens & vulnerable adults)
- Everyone in a role of trust is **Police-vetted** and, for kids/teen work,
  **safety-checked under the Children's Act 2014** (see [05](../05_hr_and_safeguarding.md)).
- **Clear boundaries:** no one-to-one seclusion with a child; no facilitator
  entering private/therapeutic territory; no dual/financial relationships with
  members.
- **Named Safeguarding Lead** and a documented **concern-reporting + escalation
  path** (including to NZ authorities — Oranga Tamariki / Police — when required).
- **Mandatory reporting** of abuse concerns.
- **Distressed participants** are supported and **referred** to professionals — we
  are not a clinical service (see [L1](01_disclaimer_and_participation_terms.md)).

## Complaints & concerns process
1. Raise it with any facilitator or the Safeguarding Lead.
2. If it concerns the founder or isn't resolved, raise it with the **independent
   advisory member**.
3. Written record kept; response within a set timeframe; serious matters escalated
   externally.
4. No retaliation, ever, for raising a concern or leaving.

## Facilitator code of conduct (the behavioural core)
- Stay in the **educator/host** lane — never diagnose, treat, or give
  professional advice.
- Hold space; don't fix, preach, or impose beliefs.
- No romantic/financial/exploitative relationships with members.
- Confidentiality is absolute (breaching it is dismissable).
- Model humility — you are not above the members.

### How to *use* this so you're never mistaken for a cult
- **Publish the charter** on the website and in the workbook.
- **Say it out loud** at intro sessions: "here's how we make sure this stays
  healthy and open."
- **Show the money** — transparency about fees and where they go disarms the #1
  cult suspicion.
- **Invite scrutiny** — guests, family, advisors. Openness is the opposite of a
  cult's secrecy.
- **Name an independent advisor early** — a single respected local person on your
  governance group changes the entire perception.
