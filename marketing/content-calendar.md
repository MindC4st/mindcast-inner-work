# MINDCAST — Social Media & Marketing Content Calendar

A 52-week content system for adults, teens and children.
Sundays · Acacia Bay Community Hall, Taupō · mindcast.co.nz

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Brand:** MINDCAST — *Tools for Intentional Living.*
**Framework:** Notice It. Name It. Do It.
**Offer:** Your first session is free.
**Pricing (for posters/socials):** Adults $19/wk · $20 casual · $150/10 sessions. Teens $9/wk · $10 casual · $50/10-session card. Children free with family.
**Audiences:** Adults (25–60), Teens (13–18), Children (5–12), Families.

**Channels & cadence:**
| Channel | Cadence | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | 2×/week (Mon + Thu) | Theme posts + community/session photos |
| Instagram Stories | 3–4×/week | Session reminders, polls, behind-the-scenes |
| Facebook | 2×/week | Longer posts, event reminders, family audience |
| Email | 1×/week (Fri) | "This Sunday" preview + recap |
| Reels | 1×/week | 20–30s framework explainers ("Notice It. Name It. Do It.") |

**Voice:** warm, plain-spoken, honest, evidence-based, community-first. No hype, no guru talk, no "manifest" language. Say what's true and say it simply. Kiwi, not corporate.

**Visual rules (from the Flow master block):** 35mm documentary feel, natural grain, muted colour, real New Zealand people and places, no uniforms in brand colours, no fake MINDCAST lettering — leave space for the official wordmark, text always on a frosted-white translucent panel.

## 2. The 52-week arc (4 blocks)

| Block | Weeks | Theme | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — See Clearly | 1–13 | Learn to notice what's actually going on | "Before you change anything, see it clearly." |
| 2 — Unlearn | 14–26 | Let go of what was never yours | "Not everything you carry is yours to keep." |
| 3 — Rebuild | 27–39 | Build it deliberately | "You get to choose what you build next." |
| 4 — Live It | 40–52 | Live it outward | "A life noticed, named and done — shared." |

## 4. 52-week content calendar

Each week: **hook** (feed post), **story/reel** idea, **email subject**.

### Block 1 — See Clearly (Weeks 1–13)

**Week 1 · The Signal and the Noise**
- Post: "Your phone has 47 tabs open. Your head's the same. This year we learn to close them and hear the quiet channel underneath. Sundays, Acacia Bay. First session free."
- Story/Reel: "Signal vs noise" — 20s split of a busy phone vs one deep breath.
- Email: "This Sunday: what are you actually receiving?"

**Week 2 · The Stories We Carry**
- Post: "Some of what you believe about yourself was written by someone else. This week we read the code you were given — and decide what to keep."
- Story/Reel: "The playlist you didn't choose" — poll: "which story about you is borrowed?"
- Email: "The stories you didn't write."

**Week 3 · The Pattern Interrupt**
- Post: "Between what happens and what you do, there's a gap. Most of us never use it. This week: find the gap."
- Story/Reel: "Pause button" — slow-motion freeze of a hand reaching for a phone, then a breath.
- Email: "The pause you keep forgetting."

**Week 4 · What Your Body Is Telling You**
- Post: "Your body reports in before your brain does. Tight shoulders, shallow breath, a gut that knows first. This week we turn the diagnostics back on."
- Story/Reel: "Where do you feel stress first?" — body-map poll.
- Email: "Your body already knows."

**Week 5 · The Mirror — How We See Ourselves**
- Post: "Most of us rate ourselves above average — and it isn't vanity, it's a blind spot. This week: recalibrate the lens."
- Story/Reel: "The true mirror" — before/after filter metaphor.
- Email: "The mirror that tells the truth."

**Week 6 · The Comparison Loop**
- Post: "You're comparing your insides to everyone's highlight reel. The race has a moving finish line. This week we step off."
- Story/Reel: "The race with no finish line" — quick cut of endless scrolling.
- Email: "Get off the loop."

**Week 7 · The Inner Critic**
- Post: "That voice in your head? It has a name and a job. This week we meet it — and learn to answer it instead of obeying it."
- Story/Reel: "Name the critic" — prompt people to comment their critic's name.
- Email: "Meet the voice in your head."

**Week 8 · Beneath the Surface — Emotions as Data**
- Post: "Anger is the lid. Underneath is something that wanted to be noticed. This week: read the feelings like data, not drama."
- Story/Reel: "The feelings iceberg" — graphic of what's under the surface.
- Email: "Feelings are information."

**Week 9 · The Wounds We Carry**
- Post: "Not everything that shaped you was your fault. But everything can be met with care. This week: look at the wounds without flinching."
- Story/Reel: "Kintsugi" — cracked, then repaired, made visible.
- Email: "This one is gentle."

**Week 10 · The Masks We Wear**
- Post: "We all have a face for the room and a face underneath. This week: notice which one you're wearing, and why."
- Story/Reel: "Frontend / backend" — the public face vs the real one.
- Email: "Take the mask off, briefly."

**Week 11 · Seeing Others Clearly**
- Post: "You don't see people — you see your assumptions about them. This week: turn down the projector and look."
- Story/Reel: "Walk around the picture" — two sides of one scene.
- Email: "See them, not the story."

**Week 12 · The Habit Loop**
- Post: "You didn't decide to be this way — you practised it. Cue, routine, reward. This week: map the loop before you break it."
- Story/Reel: "Habit path" — a path worn through long grass.
- Email: "How you got here."

**Week 13 · Integration — What We Now See**
- Post: "Thirteen weeks of looking. Here's what we've learned to see. This Sunday: take stock of the first block."
- Story/Reel: "The full backpack" — laying everything out.
- Email: "Block one, complete."
