# MINDCAST — Pre-launch campaign strategy ("TUNE IN")

TikTok-first. No start date, no venue booked yet — market the idea and the standards now, without implying dates or locations. Frame: *"Mindcast is being built — here's what it is, what it isn't, and why it will be safe and usable."* Calm and factual, no hype, no "launch soon" pressure.

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** *A weekly, in-person practice for follow-through — awareness into action, together.*

**Non-negotiables (brand + safety):**
- No urgency/scarcity, no "don't miss out", no countdowns, no guilt retention.
- No therapy/clinical framing; explicitly "not therapy / not religion / not self-help / not a podcast".
- Never imply dependency on Mindcast; celebrate portability and independence.

## 2. Message pillars (rotate weekly)

1. **Follow-through (the missing step)** — "We don't have a knowledge problem. We have a follow-through problem."
2. **The room** — weekly practice, in person, Taupō; what "structured" means.
3. **Three tracks / one theme** — adults, teens, children; same theme, different depth (whānau-wide language).
4. **Safety + boundaries** — clear scope ("not therapy"), autonomy over dependence, consent-first imagery, no parasocial founder.
5. **Signals from the curriculum** — micro-practices that stand alone ("keep what helps"), not promises.

## 3. 6-week "TUNE IN" lead-up (IG)

- Week 1 — What Mindcast is (and isn't)
- Week 2 — Follow-through > content
- Week 3 — The weekly rhythm (reflect → gather → commit)
- Week 4 — Three tracks, one theme
- Week 5 — Safety by design
- Week 6 — Founding waitlist invitation (quiet, direct, no pressure)

IG cadence: 3 posts/week + 3–7 stories. Optional TikTok 2/wk, LinkedIn 1/wk.

## 4. TikTok 30-day calendar (12 posts, Mon/Wed/Fri)

Format: 15–30s, one idea, calm face-to-camera delivery. End every post with the soft CTA:
*"If you want a weekly practice for follow-through, join the waitlist — link in bio. No pressure."*

**Week 1 — What is Mindcast?**
1. **Mon — The follow-through problem.** Hook: "We don't have a knowledge problem." → "We have a follow-through problem. Mindcast is being built as a weekly, in-person practice where you come back in seven days and face what you actually did."
2. **Wed — What it is / isn't.** Hook: "Mindcast is not therapy." → "It's a structured weekly practice for follow-through. Not therapy, not religion, not a podcast — a room that helps you turn awareness into one honest action."
3. **Fri — The "third one".** Hook: "Three versions of change." → "You're unaware and you don't change. You're aware and you still don't change. You're aware — and you do. Mindcast is built for that third one."

**Week 2 — How it works (no date yet)**
4. **Mon — The weekly rhythm.** Hook: "Here's the whole method." → "Reflect on last week. Gather in a real room. Commit to one intention you can actually do. Then come back in seven days."
5. **Wed — Why in-person.** Hook: "Why not just an app?" → "Because behaviour changes in relationships and routines. A real room creates follow-through in a way 'saved content' doesn't."
6. **Fri — One intention (almost boring).** Hook: "Your intention should be almost boring." → "One honest conversation. One 10-minute walk. One boundary sentence."

**Week 3 — Three tracks, one theme**
7. **Mon — Three tracks / one theme.** Hook: "Adults, teens, kids — same theme." → "Different depth, different words, same weekly theme — so the conversation continues in normal life, not just in a session."
8. **Wed — Build-in-public.** Hook: "Here's what I'm doing before any live sessions." → "I'm finishing the full lesson sequence first — because consistency and safety matter more than rushing."
9. **Fri — Micro-practice (portable).** Hook: "Try this once this week." → "Notice the moment you go automatic. Name it in one sentence. Do one tiny action once. Use it even if you never join Mindcast."

**Week 4 — Trust: safety-by-design + anti-hype**
10. **Mon — Marketing standards.** Hook: "A rule for my marketing." → "No countdowns. No manufactured scarcity. No guilt. If Mindcast is right for you, you'll feel that without me pushing."
11. **Wed — Anti-dependency.** Hook: "A community shouldn't replace your life." → "Mindcast shouldn't become your 'tribe' or 'family'. The goal is a stronger life outside the room — not dependence on the room."
12. **Fri — The "not therapy" boundary.** Hook: "If you need clinical support…" → "Mindcast isn't therapy. If you need clinical help, you deserve qualified support. Mindcast is a practice space for reflection, choice, and follow-through."

## 5. Pre-launch CTAs (no date/venue required)

- "Follow to watch Mindcast being built."
- "If you're in Aotearoa and this resonates, you can join the waitlist (link in bio). No pressure."
- "Comment 'WAITLIST' and I'll reply when it's ready."
- Avoid: "Launching soon", "spots limited", "don't miss out".

## 6. Scripts (word-for-word)

**Script A (20s) — What it is.** Hook: "If self-development content worked on its own, we'd all be done by now." Body: "Mindcast is a weekly, in-person practice for follow-through. You come back in seven days, review one intention, then set the next one. Not therapy. Not religion. Not a podcast." Close: "I'm building it now. Follow if you want the clear signal, not the hype."

**Script B (15s) — Micro-practice.** Hook: "Try this once this week." Body: "When you notice yourself going automatic, pause — name what's happening in one sentence — and choose one tiny action you can repeat once." Close: "Use it even if you never join Mindcast."

## 7. Caption templates (rotate)

- "A weekly practice for follow-through. Join the waitlist — link in bio."
- "No hype. No pressure. Just a clear signal. Waitlist in bio."
- "Not therapy. Not religion. A structured weekly practice. Waitlist in bio."

## 8. Visual + tone checklist

- **Design:** navy/ivory dominant; Signal Blue only as accent; whitespace + type hierarchy.
- **Language:** invite, don't hype; NZ English; correct macrons (Taupō, whānau).
- **Hard "no":** scarcity, pressure CTAs, transformation claims, dependency cues ("your tribe/family", "don't lose progress").
- **Safety:** never post identifiable participant/child content without consent; keep staff/brand separation clear.

## 9. KPI plan

- **Primary:** waitlist signups/week; landing-page conversion rate.
- **Secondary:** saves/shares (carousels), average watch time (reels), reply DMs.
- **Qualitative:** comments repeating intended language ("weekly practice", "follow-through", "not therapy").
