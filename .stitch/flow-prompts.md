## 2. Master global instruction block

Paste this at the **top of every prompt**.

```
IMPORTANT — READ THIS AS THE HIGHEST PRIORITY INSTRUCTION.

USE THE ATTACHED REFERENCE IMAGES EXACTLY AS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH.

REFERENCE IMAGE ROLES:
- Venue reference images = exact architecture, room layout, doorway shapes, windows, walls, flooring, proportions, sightlines and camera feel.
- Product reference images = exact look and styling direction for MINDCAST products on tables.
- Fashion reference images = wardrobe direction only for the people.
- Brand reference images = exact MINDCAST logo style, blue/navy/ivory branding palette, flags, signage and text styling.

DO NOT IGNORE THE REFERENCE IMAGES.
DO NOT SUBSTITUTE A GENERIC COMMUNITY HALL.
DO NOT INVENT A DIFFERENT BUILDING.
DO NOT MODERNISE OR REDESIGN THE VENUE.
DO NOT CREATE A LOOKALIKE BUILDING.
DO NOT CHANGE THE ROOM ARCHITECTURE.
DO NOT CHANGE THE CAMERA INTO A DIFFERENT VIEW UNLESS EXPLICITLY INSTRUCTED.

VENUE RULE:
Treat the attached venue image(s) as a locked photographic plate.
Preserve the actual venue identity.
Keep the building or room immediately recognisable as Great Lake Centre.

BRANDING RULE:
Only the MINDCAST branding should use the MINDCAST palette: navy, blue, ivory, white.
The PEOPLE must NOT all be dressed in brand colours.

TEXT READABILITY RULE:
All poster text must sit on a frosted-white translucent panel or frosted-white text block so the text is easy to read.
Use soft blur / frosted glass styling behind text.
Do not place dark text directly over a busy background without support.

OVERALL AESTHETIC:
modern, premium, warm, community-focused, editorial, natural, authentic, New Zealand.
Not corporate. Not stock-photo cheesy.

PEOPLE RULE:
People should look like real New Zealand adults, teenagers and children.
Natural body language. Natural posture. Not over-posed. Not symmetrical.
Not in uniform. Not all smiling directly at camera.

FASHION RULE — VERY IMPORTANT:
The current look must NOT become a MINDCAST "uniform".
Do NOT dress everyone in navy, blue, grey, beige, cream or coordinated neutrals.
Create real outfit variety using the attached fashion references.

ADULT WOMEN — wardrobe direction:
- wide-leg blue denim with burgundy T-shirt and relaxed grey blazer
- black wide-leg trousers with white T-shirt and charcoal cropped jacket
- light-wash jeans with oversized cream or grey knit

PHOTOGRAPHY:
35mm documentary/editorial feel, natural grain, muted colour, realistic imperfect light,
ordinary New Zealand environment. Not glossy. Not HDR. Not corporate stock photography.
Not luxury wellness advertising.

NEGATIVE:
no generated logos, no readable text, no watermarks, no candles, no crystals,
no meditation poses, no raised hands, no people holding hands, no spiritual staging,
no guru imagery, no glossy stock-photo smiles, no matching outfits, no brand-colour uniforms.

BRAND / TEXT RULE:
DO NOT try to redraw the MINDCAST wordmark.
DO NOT generate fake MINDCAST lettering.
DO NOT generate the microphone logo.
Leave clean intentional space where the official transparent MINDCAST wordmark can be composited afterwards in Canva or Figma.
Where poster copy will eventually go, create a blank FROSTED-WHITE translucent panel:
warm ivory-white, approximately 88–94% opacity, subtle background blur, soft feathered edge,
premium editorial appearance, completely blank, no generated text inside it.
```

## 3. The seven prompts

### 1) Hero entrance poster (main launch)

```
[PASTE MASTER GLOBAL INSTRUCTION BLOCK ABOVE THIS LINE]

CREATE A SINGLE POSTER IMAGE.

SCENE TYPE: MINDCAST hero entrance poster.

PRIMARY REFERENCE PRIORITY:
1. Attached exterior / entrance photos of Great Lake Centre
2. Attached MINDCAST branding references
3. Attached product references
4. Attached fashion references

VERY IMPORTANT — COMPOSITION RULE:
This must be ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS PHOTOGRAPH.
Do NOT create a split-screen, side-by-side collage, multiple panels, diptych,
a cutaway building, or a second interior frame.
The viewer must feel they are standing outside the REAL Great Lake Centre looking toward the real entrance.

VENUE PRESERVATION:
Keep the exact building identity from the attached entrance photos — same roof shape,
roof pitch, façade proportions, brickwork, doors, windows, entrance proportions,
path and garden context, camera feel. Do NOT redesign the building.

DOOR / FOYER INSTRUCTION:
The front entrance doors should be open. Through the open doors, the viewer sees a believable
glimpse into the foyer. Inside the foyer, visible through the doorway, place a MINDCAST
welcome/product table, naturally positioned 2–4 metres inside the entrance (not a separate room scene).
Do not enlarge the doorway unrealistically. Do not redesign the architecture to fit the table.

PRODUCT TABLE:
Use the attached MINDCAST product images as the exact reference direction.
Show neatly displayed: 90 Day Planner, Life Binder, Reflection Journal, Folio, worksheets,
pens, family tools / intention tools. Premium but practical, on a timber or warm neutral welcome table.

PEOPLE OUTSIDE:
A natural Sunday arrival scene with adults, teens and children. Examples:
2 adult women walking in conversation; 1 adult man arriving with a child; 1 teen boy walking
slightly behind; 1 teen girl near the entrance; 1 parent holding a child's hand; 1 or 2 people
already visible inside. People staggered naturally along the path and near the entrance.
No staff lineup. No symmetrical posing.

FLAGS / SIGNAGE:
Place MINDCAST feather flags outside near the entrance. Use the attached branding references.
Keep branding premium, clean and minimal.
```

## 4. "Don't get it wrong" block

Append to any prompt if Flow keeps drifting:

```
FAILURES TO AVOID:
- Do not generate a different building.
- Do not modernise the hall.
- Do not split the image into two scenes.
- Do not create a collage.
- Do not place interior and exterior as separate photographs.
- Do not put the audience facing random directions.
- Do not coordinate everyone's outfits into brand colours.
- Do not dress attendees like staff.
- Do not replace the products with generic books or folders.
- Do not ignore the attached fashion / venue / product references.
- Do not place text directly on a busy background without a frosted-white support panel.
```
